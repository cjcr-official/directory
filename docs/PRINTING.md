# Getting it out of the printer

The layout is fixed to landscape paper split down the middle: **three records on
each half, six to a sheet**. What changes is how you fold and bind it.

---

## Decide this first: booklet or flat?

### Flat (leave booklet order off)

Pages run 1, 2, 3, 4… straight through. Print single- or double-sided, staple
the corner, done. Choose this for:

- reading on a screen or emailing the PDF
- a quick handout for an event
- anything you are not going to fold

### Booklet (turn booklet order on)

Pages are shuffled so that when the whole stack is printed double-sided, folded
in half and stapled through the spine, they read 1, 2, 3, 4… in your hand. This
is the real church directory: a 5½ × 8½ inch booklet that sits in a glovebox.

The preview will look scrambled. That is correct — page 1 sits next to the last
page on the same sheet, because that is where they land once folded.

**Printer settings that matter**

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Paper | Letter (or A4), **landscape** |
| Two-sided | On — **flip on the short edge** |
| Scale | 100% / "Actual size" — **not** "Fit to page" |
| Margins | None / default |
| Booklet mode in the printer driver | **Off** — the PDF is already imposed |

Flipping on the long edge instead of the short one prints every other page
upside down. If that happens, that is the setting to change.

Do not let the printer do its own booklet imposition on top of this one. Turning
both on shuffles the pages twice.

---

## Two ways to print

**Download PDF** builds a real file in your browser. Use this to email it to a
print shop, keep a copy, or print later.

**Print** goes straight to the browser's print dialog from the preview. Faster,
and fine for a handful of copies. Set the same options as above, and turn off
"Headers and footers" so the browser does not stamp a URL across your directory.

---

## How much paper?

Six records to a sheet, plus a cover and an index.

| Records | Sheets, double-sided |
| --- | --- |
| 30 | 3 |
| 60 | 6 |
| 120 | 11 |
| 250 | 22 |

The preview bar tells you the real number for your directory.

If you are having it printed commercially, ask for **saddle-stitched, folded to
half letter**, and send the booklet-ordered PDF. If the shop would rather impose
it themselves, send the flat version instead and tell them so.

---

## Making it fit

If a card looks crowded:

- **Text size → Compact** buys about 10% more room on every card.
- Turn off details you do not need. Email addresses are the longest thing on a
  card and often the least used on paper.
- **Member style → One line of first names** is much tighter than a line each.

If it looks sparse:

- **Text size → Large** is genuinely easier on older eyes, and worth doing
  before anything else.
- Two records per half instead of three gives each family a lot more room, at
  the cost of half again as much paper.

## Photographs

Photos are cropped to a matching upright rectangle by default, which is what
makes a page look tidy when the pictures arrive at a dozen different sizes and
angles. If a crop cuts someone badly, switch that directory to **Show the whole
photo** — every photo is then letterboxed instead, and nothing is cut.

Photos are shrunk in the browser when you add them, to about 900 pixels on the
long edge. That is well beyond what prints at this size, and it keeps uploads
fast and PDFs small.

Records without a photograph print with initials in a soft box. A directory
where a few faces are missing still looks deliberate.
